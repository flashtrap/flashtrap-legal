# Privacy Policy

**App:** FlashTrap: Prank Master
**Package:** com.flashtrap.prankmaster
**Contact:** flashtrap.app@gmail.com
**Last updated:** August 1, 2026

> This policy covers **FlashTrap: Prank Master** only. FlashTrap: Broken Screen Prank has its own
> privacy policy, linked from the [legal index](../).

## 1. Summary

FlashTrap: Prank Master is an entertainment app. You set up a prank on your own phone, hand it to
someone you know, and the app records their reaction so you can edit and keep the video.

Almost nothing leaves your phone. Your recordings, your edits, your exported videos and your Emoji
balance are stored locally. The only time a video of yours is uploaded anywhere is if you personally
choose to submit one to FlashTrapTV, which is an opt in action described in section 5.

## 2. Permissions we ask for, and why

* **Camera.** To show the live preview during the prank and to record the reaction video. The camera
  feed is never streamed off the device.
* **Microphone.** To record audio as part of the reaction video. Audio is never streamed off the
  device.
* **Storage** (Android 9 and older only). To save finished videos into your device gallery. Newer
  Android versions do not need this permission and the app does not request it there.

## 3. What is stored on your device and nowhere else

* Recorded reaction videos, intermediate clips and exported videos.
* Your prank settings, presets and imported prank packages.
* Your Emoji balance and progress. For anti abuse reasons this specific value is excluded from cloud
  backup, so it is lost if you uninstall the app.

Face detection, used to place effects over a face, runs entirely on your device using Google
MediaPipe. No image, video frame or face data is uploaded for this, ever.

## 4. What we receive from you

Nothing, unless you send it. We have no accounts, no login and no user profiles. We do not collect
your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location.

## 5. FlashTrapTV submissions (opt in)

If you choose to submit a video to FlashTrapTV, that video file, including its audio, is uploaded to
our cloud storage so that we can consider featuring it on our social media channels.

* This only happens when you explicitly choose to submit a video and agree to the submission terms.
  It never happens automatically or in the background.
* Uploads are stored using **Dropbox**, which acts as our storage provider. Dropbox's own privacy
  policy applies to their handling of that file: https://www.dropbox.com/privacy
* Submitted videos are transmitted over an encrypted connection.
* We keep submitted videos only for as long as we need them to review and potentially publish them.
  You can ask us to delete yours at any time. See section 8.

By submitting, you confirm that everyone appearing in the video has agreed to it being shared
publicly. See section 7 and our Terms of Service.

## 6. Advertising

The app includes Google AdMob to show occasional advertisements. AdMob may use your device's
advertising ID to select and measure ads. We do not receive that identifier ourselves and we do not
combine it with anything else.

* Google's advertising policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
* You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings under Privacy, Ads.

## 7. Recording other people

This app exists to record someone's reaction, which means it records a person other than you.

You are responsible for that. Only prank people you know, who would find it funny, and who you are
confident will be comfortable with the recording. Do not use this app to record anyone covertly, to
harass anyone, or in any place or situation where recording is not permitted. Recording laws differ
from country to country and, in the United States, from state to state.

If you submit a video to FlashTrapTV, you are confirming that you have the agreement of everyone who
appears in it.

## 8. Deleting your data

* **On your device:** delete individual videos inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove
  everything it stored locally.
* **Videos you submitted to FlashTrapTV:** email **flashtrap.app@gmail.com** and ask us to delete
  your submission. Tell us roughly when you sent it and what it showed so we can find it. We will
  delete it within 30 days. You do not need an account to make this request.

## 9. Third party services we actually use

This is the complete list:

* **Google Play Services**, for core app functionality.
* **Google AdMob**, for advertising (section 6).
* **Google Play In App Review**, to show the standard Android rating prompt.
* **Dropbox**, as storage for FlashTrapTV submissions only (section 5).

We do **not** use any analytics SDK, and we do **not** use any crash reporting service. Nothing in
this app reports your usage back to us.

## 10. Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any personal
information from them. If you believe a child has submitted a video to us, contact us at the address
below and we will delete it.

## 11. Security

Uploads are encrypted in transit. We limit our own storage access to the single folder that receives
submissions. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee
absolute security.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and changes take effect
when posted on this page.
